Shape: the chord shapes as printed in the tab. Key: how the song sounds = shape + capo. Set the capo (key is computed) or set the key (capo is computed).

Print

Options

Text is sized to fit the width, between min and max; lines that don't fit at the minimum wrap.
Booklet sheets are shown the way they print: imposed for folding, so a sheet's pages are not in reading order (e.g. pages 4 | 1 on the front of sheet 1; page 4 is the back cover). Switch View to Reading order to see the pages as a book.
Print: paper A4 landscape, scale 100%, margins none, headers/footers off. Booklets: duplex, flip on the short edge, fold along the dashed line. "Save as PDF" in the print dialog gives you a file.
Edit tab
Left column = how each line is read: H section header · C chords · L lyrics · T tab · M note/metadata · · blank · ⚠ contains invisible characters. If a line is not read the way you expect, fix it here.
